# Support

## Getting Help

- **Documentation:** Start with [`README.md`](README.md), [`LEGAL.md`](LEGAL.md), and the model & plugin guides in [`docs/`](docs/).
- **Bug reports & feature requests:** Open an issue using the repository templates at <https://github.com/spencernorton/armiger/issues/new/choose>.
- **Security or safety issues:** Follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).
- **Hugging Face Hub:** Official model cards and weights are also published under the [NorviTech Hugging Face organization](https://huggingface.co/NorviTech).
